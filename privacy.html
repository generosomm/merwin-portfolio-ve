Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

The short version

generosomm.dev is the personal portfolio of Merwin Generoso. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. It does not collect, store or sell information about the people who visit it.

Data about visitors

  • No cookies or trackers. The site sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts.
  • Browser storage. The site saves one small flag in your browser's session storage so the intro screen shows only once per visit. It never leaves your device and is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers briefly record standard request details (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. I don't use these logs to identify visitors. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Contact form. If you send me a project brief, the form opens your own email app with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app, and the message then reaches me as a normal email. I use it only to reply to you.

Connected social accounts

The site shows live statistics from my own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts. To do this, I connected those accounts to the site once, using each platform's official login and API:

  • What is read: public profile details (display name, handle, profile picture), aggregate counts (followers, likes, posts, views) and my public posts' titles, thumbnails, links and view counts.
  • Where it is stored: the access tokens the platforms issue are kept in a private database that only the site's server code can read. They are never sent to your browser. The site keeps the latest stats and one daily total per platform for trend charts.
  • Who it is about: only my own accounts. The site does not read, store or show information about anyone else, including people who follow or comment on my posts. Visitors cannot log in to the site with any social account.
  • How to stop it: I can disconnect any account at any time from that platform's settings, which immediately revokes the site's access.

Use of these platforms' data follows their own terms, including the TikTok Terms of Service, the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy, and the Meta Platform Terms.

Changes and contact

If the way the site handles data changes, I will update this page and the date at the top. Questions? Email generosomerwin10@gmail.com.
